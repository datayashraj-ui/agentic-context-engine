## Approval Format

When CEO needs a decision, it sends this to Telegram:
```
❓ DECISION NEEDED: [ID]

[Context: what's happening]

My recommendation: [what CEO suggests]

Options:
A) [option A] — [consequence]
B) [option B] — [consequence]

Reply 'A', 'B', or tell me what to do.
```

Founder replies and CEO logs the outcome here.
